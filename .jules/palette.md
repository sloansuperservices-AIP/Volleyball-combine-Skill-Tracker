## 2025-05-15 - Accessible Controls for Emojis and Icon-Only Buttons
**Learning:** Emojis (e.g. 🏐, 🔒, ⚠️, 📝) and symbols (e.g., ×, ✕) used as standalone buttons or inline status indicators are completely unintelligible to screen readers without descriptive `aria-label` or `role` attributes, and they lack clear tooltips for visual assistance.
**Action:** Always apply descriptive `aria-label` and `title` attributes on standalone icon-only buttons or status icons to guarantee accessibility compatibility and professional micro-UX tooltip guidance.
